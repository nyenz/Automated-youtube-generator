Props

The prop sheet is rougher in the original (scratches and specks), so its clean version keeps a little of that texture. Props drawn clean from the start will come out as clean as the people.

What this means

  1. The look comes from images in your style (made once, with any image AI), and the app turns them into clean vector shapes automatically.
  2. Vector means every part can move: heads turn, bodies breathe, arms and legs can be posed. That is how the videos get made.
  3. Bigger sources give cleaner results. Characters around 1000 pixels tall trace much better than the small headshots on your sheets.
  4. Real brand logos (the swooshes) are removed before anything goes into a video.
