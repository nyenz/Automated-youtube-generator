The Longest Fall

How Betty Lou Oliver survived a 75-floor elevator fall in the Empire State Building in 1945. Captions follow a timed narration; the real voice would come from Google AI Studio in the app. Click any line to jump to it.

    Facts: 28 July 1945, a B-25 bomber hit the 79th floor in fog; 14 people died. Betty, 20, fell about 75 floors and survived with a broken pelvis, back and neck, and went back to work five months later. Source: MigFlug, "The Bomber That Hit the Empire State".
