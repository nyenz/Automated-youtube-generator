# Style 02 — Style Bible (from Nyenz's reference sheets, 11 Oct 2026)

Reference sheets: characters (lineup + turnarounds + headshots + poses) and three prop sheets
(buildings, clothes, accessories, furniture, sports, bags, tech, food, tools, vehicles).
Numbers below were measured from the images (colour clusters, background, dark share), not guessed.

## 0. What makes it look "clean"
1. Big simple shapes, few of them. A whole character is ~25–40 flat shapes, not hundreds.
2. Exactly two tones per material (base + one shadow). No gradients, no blur, no glow, no rim light, no grain, no vignette.
3. No outlines. Edges are made by colour contrast. Thin dark strokes appear only *inside* shapes for seams, pockets, laces, eyelids.
4. One shared warm palette. Every colour is pulled toward warm brown, so even teal, purple and pink sit together.
5. Detail is spent only where the eye goes: face, hands, shoes, one logo/print. Everything else stays plain.
6. Lots of near-black. About 40% of all drawn pixels are very dark (hair, black clothes, shadows, soles). The dark masses frame the colours and make it read graphic.

## 1. Two families in the sheets (important)
| | Characters (sheets 4–5) | Props (sheets 1–3) |
|---|---|---|
| Finish | Clean flat vector | Painted, gritty, chipped edges, speckles |
| Tones per material | 2 | 3–4 (base, dark side, light chip, speck) |
| View | 3/4 front, profile, 3/4 back | 3/4 from slightly above, all the same angle |
| Edges | Crisp | Rough, dark shadow side acts like an outline |

**Rule for our kit:** characters stay 100% clean. Props use the character rules **plus** one light "wear" tone used sparingly (a few chips on edges and corners), so they look like the same world but slightly lived-in. No speckle noise.

## 2. Canvas
- Background: one flat warm khaki. Measured **#AB9370 → #B0997C** (≈ `#AE9675`). No texture, no gradient.
- Ground shadow under people/props: a short flat horizontal strip, darker khaki-brown (≈ `#4E4236`, ~70% opacity), slightly offset to the right, about as wide as the stance plus a bit. Not an ellipse, no blur.
- Headings/labels: hand-lettered uppercase marker style with a ruled underline (model-sheet look). Only for sheets, never in videos.

## 3. Colour system (measured)
**Neutrals**
- Ink black (hair, pupils, lid lines, soles, deepest shadow): `#050304` (warm near-black, never pure #000).
- Soft black for black *clothing*: base `#332C32` / shadow `#1F1920`. Black clothes are never ink; the ink is darker so hair still reads on a black hoodie.
- Cream instead of white: `#F5ECE4`, `#EEE0D0`. Pure white is not used. Cream shadow: `#C9B6A8`–`#B58A86` (pinkish).

**Skin** (always warm and slightly red; shadow shifts toward red-magenta and darker)
| Tone | Base | Shadow | Blush / lips |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep | `#7A2E28` (measured #822B26, #70352C) | `#4A1A1E` (#4E2022, #48161A) | slightly darker red dots |
| Brown | `#8F4A30` | `#5A2A1C` | — |
| Tan | `#C47A50` | `#8E4E34` | `#A75941` |
| Light | `#E69972` (measured) | `#B86A4E` | `#E57A56` cheek patch |
| Fair (elderly) | `#E8A88A` | `#B87862` | rosy `#E57A56` |
Skin is never grey-beige; even the lightest skin is peach-orange.

**Clothing colours seen (base / shadow)**
- Teal jersey `#057378` / `#034F55` · denim `#527481` / `#284150` · orange cap `#E13B07` / `#A82A05`
- Purple `#6D3771` / `#431648` · deep purple `#643571` / `#391A35` · yellow `#FAAA30` / `#DB7F22`
- Red `#C31E28` / `#76111A` · olive `#5D6B3A` / `#3B4524` · khaki pants `#A08A68` / `#6F5D45` · pink beanie `#D0408A` / `#8E2A62`
- Props accents: brick red `#C31E28`, orange `#E49645`, deep teal `#125660`, indigo `#4E4D7E`, brown leather `#957052` / `#69563F`.

**Palette rules**
1. Saturation is medium (average ~0.42). Only one or two accents per character are strong (cap, beanie, hoodie); the rest are muted.
2. Shadows are darker **and** shift hue: blue → navy-teal, purple → wine, yellow → orange, skin → red-brown. Never make a shadow by just adding black.
3. On clothing the shadow tone often covers **as much or more** area than the base (pants: shadow 21% vs base 11%). The lit part is the smaller, "designed" shape.
4. Every character has one dominant colour (hoodie/jacket/pants) + black + cream + skin. That's it.

## 4. Light and shadow
- Light comes from the front-left and slightly above. Shadows fall on the far side of the body, under the shirt/hoodie hem, inside sleeves, between the legs, under the cap brim, under the chin.
- Shadows are **hard, angular shapes**: folds are drawn as sharp triangles and zig-zags (cargo pants have 3–5 big angular fold shapes from knee to ankle). Not offset copies of the outline (that is what our current kit does, and why it looks mechanical).
- No rim light (our pink rim is not in the references — remove it).
- No highlights on skin. Occasionally a lighter patch on top surfaces of props only.

## 5. Shapes and edges
- Slightly angular: curves are made of a few flat-ish segments with softened corners, like cut vinyl. Hair tips, fabric hems and pockets end in sharp points.
- Big masses first: hoodie = one block, pants = one big trapezoid per leg, sneaker = 3 stacked blocks (sole, midsole, upper).
- Small parts are tiny and few: earrings (gold hoops `#E2A052`), a chain, a drawstring, 2–3 lace marks.

## 6. Proportions
| Type | Height in heads | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Teen / young adult | 6–6.5 | head small, neck thin, shoulders narrow (≈1.3 head widths) |
| Adult | 6.5–7 | slightly broader, longer garments |
| Elderly | 5.5–6 | hunched forward, shorter, glasses, cane or bag |
| Child | 4–4.5 | big head, short legs, shorts + tall socks |
- Feet/shoes are huge and chunky: one shoe ≈ 1 head long, ≈ 0.45 head tall.
- Pants are very wide: at the hem each leg is ≈ 1.2–1.5 head widths and pools over the shoe (only the toe and sole show).
- Tops are oversized: sleeves past the wrist, hoodie hem at mid-thigh, shoulders drop.
- Arms long and thin; hands small, usually hidden in pockets.
- Torso short vs legs (legs ≈ 50–55% of height including shoes).

## 7. Pose and attitude
- Default stance: relaxed slouch — hips pushed forward, shoulders back, head slightly forward, weight on one leg (contrapposto), one knee bent.
- Hands in pockets is the default (also saves animation). Other poses: hand on bag strap, holding phone, peace sign, skateboard, cane.
- Default view is **3/4 front**. Turnarounds: 3/4 front, profile, 3/4 back. Walking: big stride, shoulders sway, slight bounce.
- Pose ideas row: sitting on a crate, crouching, leaning, skating, sitting on the floor — always loose and casual.

## 8. Face (the signature)
- **Eyes:** half-lidded, "unimpressed/side-eye". The upper lid is a flat straight line cutting across the eye white, drawn with a thick ink lash line that extends past the outer corner (a small flick). Eye whites are cream, shaped like a half-moon (flat top, round bottom). Pupils are small ink dots pushed toward the side the character looks.
- **Brows:** thick short ink slabs, low and close to the eyes, slightly angled. Brows carry most of the emotion.
- **Nose:** varies by person: big rounded "bulb" that breaks the silhouette for some (Black male, Black female), small pointed for others (white/Asian male), medium rounded for most. In 3/4 view the nose overlaps the cheek line.
- **Mouth:** tiny — a short smirk line near one corner, sometimes a slightly darker lip shape. Never a big open mouth at rest.
- **Ears:** simple C shape with one inner shadow; gold hoops common.
- **Cheeks:** a soft blush patch on light skin; 2–3 tiny freckle/dots under the eye on many characters.
- **Head shape:** rounded skull, short soft jaw, small chin; the face sits low on the head.
- **Expressions** in the headshot rows are subtle: lid height, pupil direction, brow angle and a tiny mouth change. No huge cartoon mouths.

## 9. Hair (graphic black masses)
- Afro: cloud with scalloped/lumpy edge, big (≈1.5× head width), 1 tone + a few darker curl marks.
- Dreads/locs: beaded segments (chains of rounded pieces), hang behind ears.
- Straight long: flat sheets with pointed tips and a couple of inner parting lines.
- Spiky (Asian male): jagged anime-style flame tips.
- Short curly (kid): tight clumpy cap.
- Grey (elderly): `#9A9A9E` / `#6F6E74` with a bun.
- Blonde: `#E8D08A` / `#C4A45E` sheets.
- Hair under hats always shows at the sides/back.

## 10. Clothes and accessories (vocabulary)
- Streetwear: oversized hoodies (black, red, grey), varsity jackets, jerseys with big numbers, crop tops, cargo pants with big low side pockets (rectangle + flap), baggy jeans, long coats.
- Stripes: two thick bands on sleeves (cream + black).
- Sneakers everywhere (even the elderly and the thobe/abaya): cream midsole, dark sole, coloured panels, laces as 2–3 marks, white socks peeking.
- Bags: black backpacks, crossbody bags, messenger bags; straps are simple dark bands across the body.
- Hats: caps (worn forward, backward or high), beanies, bucket hats, keffiyeh, hijab.
- Jewellery: gold hoops, thin chains, a watch.

## 11. Diversity system (what changes, what stays)
Changes per person: skin tone, hair type and colour, nose shape, garment culture (thobe + keffiyeh, abaya + hijab), age cues (grey hair, beard, glasses, cane, posture, height), body width, accessories.
Never changes: the eye style, the brow slab, the 2-tone shading, the warm palette, the chunky sneakers, the slouchy attitude. That shared skeleton is what makes 16 very different people look like one cast.

## 12. Props and places
- One camera angle for all objects: 3/4 view from slightly above (≈20–30° down), front-left.
- Buildings are "diorama" pieces: each sits on its own small slab of pavement/grass, with tiny trees (round blob clusters), street lights, signs, awnings (red/cream stripes). Lots of small windows, warm lit windows, red signage.
- Furniture and objects: chunky, slightly worn, dark sides, star emblem as the house "brand" motif on many items (bags, cups, bottles, caps).
- Food is the most saturated thing in the world (red, yellow).
- **Do not copy real brands** (the sheets show swooshes, cola and energy-drink logos, "Jordan"-like shoes). Our kit uses the star emblem and invented marks only.

## 13. What our current kit does wrong (gap list)
1. Profile-only characters. The references live in 3/4 front.
2. Shading made by offsetting the whole shape → uniform crescents. Needs designed angular shadow shapes per garment.
3. Pink rim light everywhere → not in the style. Remove.
4. Grain, vignette, colour-grade overlays, glow, light beams → make it look dirty. Remove for this style.
5. Eyes: big round whites with round pupils and a separate lid line. Needs half-moon whites, flat ink lid line with flick, small side pupils, thick low brows.
6. Same big nose on everyone. Needs 3–4 nose types.
7. Head too big for adults (≈1/4.5). Needs ≈1/6–1/6.5.
8. Clothes not oversized enough; pants not wide/pooled; shoes good size but need the 3-block sneaker build.
9. Hands as mitten blobs in the open. References hide them in pockets most of the time.
10. Ground shadow ellipse → flat strip.
11. Too many colours and saturated set pieces in scenes. Needs the warm khaki world + one accent per character.
