BWalk cycle

Twelve drawings per step pair, held for two frames each (animators call this "on twos"). Heel strike, squash, passing, push off. The lowest foot always stays on the ground, so the body bobs by itself. The dreads swing a moment after the head.

CFaces

The same eight emotions as style 01, so shot forms work in both styles. Brows, lids, pupils and mouth change; the head shape never does.

DCast from one builder

Five skin tones, five hair types (dreads, short, bun, afro, bald), cap or no cap, outfit colours, jersey number, body width and nose size. Real people in true stories can be matched by skin, hair and clothes.

ERules of the style

  1. Flat shapes only. No outlines, no gradients.
  2. Light comes from the front left. Each part gets one shadow shape on its back and bottom edge.
  3. A thin dusty-pink rim light sits on the back edge of big shapes. It is the signature of the style.
  4. Black is for hair, stripes, soles, pupils and brows, never for outlines.
  5. Big head, big nose, big shoes, short legs, loose clothes.
  6. Things further from the camera are darker (far arm, far leg, far shoe in pink).
  7. One warm sand background. Props are simpler and flatter than the characters.
  8. A light grain over everything, and movement on twos (12 drawings a second).
